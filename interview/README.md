## Practice Loop

For every case study:

1. Spend 5 minutes clarifying the problem.
2. State scale and quality assumptions.
3. Design for 30 minutes without notes.
4. Spend 5 minutes on failure modes and trade-offs.
5. Score the answer with the rubric.
6. Reattempt the design after several days with a new constraint.
